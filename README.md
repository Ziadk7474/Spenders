<h1 align="center">am. nexus</h1>
<p align="center">Purchase, sales, payments and cheques for <b>Al Madhi Carpentry</b> — in one fast app for PC and phone.</p>

---

## What it does
- **Bills and invoices** — purchase bills and sales invoices with line items, KG per bag, VAT and PO numbers.
- **Payments** — paid now or on credit, part payments, and invoice-wise allocation.
- **Party ledgers** — vendor and customer statements in English and Urdu, ready to print or share.
- **Cheque register** — due dates, reminders on the phone, and a view-only cheque list.
- **Quotations** — on the company letterhead.
- **Works offline** — saves are kept on the device and sent when the internet is back.

## Made for the keyboard
Every entry can be done without the mouse, like Tally.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| Enter / Tab | Next field |
| Shift (tap) / Shift+Tab | Back one field |
| `ca` / `cr` | Cash or credit |
| Alt+S | Past rates of this item — this party and everyone else |
| Alt+A / Alt+X | Add / remove a line |
| Ctrl+A or Ctrl+S | Save |
| Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y | Undo / redo |
| Esc | Close the window or go back |

Press the keyboard button at the top right of the app to see the full list.
